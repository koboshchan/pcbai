What this model includes

Nominal fitted resistors only. Each named net is ideal copper. Capacitors, diodes, op-amps and other ICs are open. Connectors never short different nets. No external boards, bodges, power supplies or meter diode effects.

A real unpowered meter may differ because of IC protection paths, capacitors, tolerances or a bad solder joint. Remove power before probing.
