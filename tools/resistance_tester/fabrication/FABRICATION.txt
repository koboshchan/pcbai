SINGLE SIDED board, BOTTOM copper only.

the KiCad Gerber-job auto metadata therefore says two layers and is NOT
an ordering specification. It is intentionally excluded from the fab ZIP.
Order single-sided bottom copper explicitly. Plating is not required for
home etching: solder through-hole components from the copper side.

Board thickness 1.6 mm. THT drill sizes are encoded in fingerprint.drl.
All 26 resistors and 26 capacitors are 0805 on B.Cu.
Only the four female headers are through-hole (66 total 1.0 mm drills).
Socket bodies are on top, Pico USB at the marked end (board +Y edge in the top view; bottom of this board when viewed from the bottom with the DUT block on the left).
Do not scale the output. Confirm final outline 100 x 70 mm with fabricator.
