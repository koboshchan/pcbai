ADS1115 home-etch carrier

This carrier is exactly 50 x 70 mm. All conductive pads and tracks are on F.Cu. There are no vias, no B.Cu copper, and no ground pour. Signal tracks are 0.5 mm, supply/ground tracks 0.8 mm. The minimum specified copper and board-edge clearance is 0.5 mm. Header/socket pads are 1.8 mm with 1.0 mm drills; four mounting holes are 3.2 mm for M3 hardware.

The three module sockets match the common blue module pin order VDD, GND, SCL, SDA, ADDR, ALRT, A0, A1, A2, A3. Pin 1 is the square VDD pad at the right end when viewed from the component side. Confirm the printing on your actual modules before plugging them in. This order was checked against the module photograph and table at https://how2electronics.com/how-to-use-ads1115-16-bit-adc-module-with-arduino/ . The drawn module envelopes are approximate, not a dimensioned guarantee for every clone.

JM1 uses address 0x48 with ADDR grounded, JM2 uses 0x49 with ADDR tied to VDD, and JM3 uses 0x4A with ADDR tied to SDA. ALRT is unused. JM1 A0-A3 connect to ADC_0-ADC_3; JM2 connects ADC_4-ADC_7; JM3 A0/A1 connect ADC_8/ADC_9, while A2/A3 are grounded. Each ADS1115 multiplexes its inputs, so the board does not acquire all ten channels simultaneously.

JAD is a 1x12 right-angle female socket at the left edge. Its opening faces the left board edge. The carrier stands perpendicular to the motherboard when plugged into its vertical male JAD. Pin 1 is the square pad, marked 1; pins 1-10 are ADC_0 through ADC_9, and 11-12 are ground. Align pin numbers, not just connector edges. This is not an upside-down parallel stacking connector. Check physical clearance over neighboring motherboard parts before installing.

JI2C pin order is 3V3, GND, SDA, SCL. Supply regulated 3.3 V from the MCU/Pico, never 5 V. JAR, marked TO JAR, is 3V3 then GND and feeds the motherboard clamp rail. The motherboard JAR pin 1 is +3V3_ADC, pin 2 GND. All boards need common ground. The motherboard's ADC channels are already divided/clamped; this carrier adds no divider.

C1 is an optional 100 nF through-hole bypass capacitor, initially DNP. The modules normally include their own bypassing and I2C pullups. No additional pullups are fitted; inspect the modules' pullup values before combining three sets on one bus.

With F.Cu on the component side, ordinary socket bodies hide their solder joints. Raise the sockets sufficiently to reach every joint, using a removable spacer, and inspect for shorts. These are single-sided hand-drilled pads, not plated through holes. Insulated wire links are required where documented in connectivity.json; they reuse existing connector holes, so they add no hand-drilling. Coincident copper landing markers model the two reused pads as electrically connected by the populated wire in KiCad, not by invisible copper. A bare etched board is not electrically complete until every link is fitted. Fit links on the underside to avoid socket/module bodies, leave slack, and insulate crossings. The 3D rendering does not show these wires or the purchased blue modules.

Fit exactly five underside insulated wire links. W1 joins JAD pin 8 to JM2 pin 10 for ADC_7, 11.01 mm center to center. W2 joins JAD pin 1 to JM1 pin 7 for ADC_0, 18.01 mm. W3 joins JAD pin 4 to JM1 pin 10 for ADC_3, 19.77 mm. W4 joins JM2 pin 1 to JM2 pin 5 for 3V3, 10.16 mm. W5 joins JM2 pin 4 to JI2C pin 3 for SDA, 14.22 mm. These are distances, not wire cut lengths; allow extra for bends and soldering. The router's best of thirty net-order trials needed five links, not a proof that five is globally minimal.

KiCad's through-hole-pad model expands copper layer membership on load. This board removes unused copper layers and does not retain outer layers, so its B.Cu plot is truly empty. Three short tracks entirely inside the unused ALRT pads retain those pads on F.Cu without wiring ALRT to another signal. Verify the included empty B.Cu Gerber rather than pad membership flags. The photographic mask contains every physical top pad. The checked design has 0 DRC errors and 0 unconnected with links fitted; the raw report retains 28 nonfatal silkscreen/text/dangling warnings, since no printed silkscreen is required for home etching. F.Cu is mirrored; copper is white/clear, the rest of the page is black. Pads contain 0.3 mm black drill-centering dots.
