Rendering commands

Run bash /home/kit/ads_render.sh BOARD only after final validation. The script uses genuine KiCad CLI top and bottom views at 2000 x 2800, checks the board hash before and after, and copies images into /home/kit/renders/ads1115_breakout/. These views show the supplied footprint models and KiCad's default soldermask rendering. The etched board should be bare copper, so the rendered surface finish is illustrative. The local render environment supplies the official KiCad STEP models through KICAD10_3DMODEL_DIR=/home/kit/kicad-models. The THT socket and header models are rendered on their real B-side footprints. C1 is shown as a modeled optional part even though the assembly instructions initially mark it DNP. Purchased ADS1115 modules and seven hand-installed insulated wire links are not modeled; the thin drawing-layer lines are only link annotations, not physical wire models. No board modifications are made for rendering.
