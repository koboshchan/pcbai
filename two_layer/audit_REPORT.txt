Important pre-existing electrical/model findings follow. These are not permission to alter the conversion's original netlist.

Every actual motherboard ADC divider has 10k top from SCORE_n to SCORE_DIV_n and 20k bottom from SCORE_DIV_n to GND. Python calculation gives ratio 2/3, +10.5V score becomes +7V, and -10.5V score becomes -7V. README's asserted 22k/10k protection is not in these footprints. There are no motherboard diode footprints protecting this interface. The emulator applies np.clip(raw_divider_voltage,0,3.3), which invents safe ADC voltages without modeling physical clamps or injection current. Even a correctly sized positive divider would not protect a negative score or an unpowered MCU by itself. Do not attach a microcontroller ADC directly on the basis of README's safe claim.

weights_layer1.json marks 563 populated synapses negative and weights_layer2.json marks 83 negative. Actual user Description fields on their resistor footprints are blank, so emulator defaults polarity to +1. R1_1_0, a nominal -0.57845 weight with 18k resistor, has IN_1/INV_N0 pads; positive R1_0_0 uses IN_0/INV_N0. TL074 noninverting pins are GND on DC1A. R2_0_0, a nominal -0.75605 weight with 12k resistor, has ACT_0/INV_L2_0 pads. These examples lack an inverted input or signed differential summing implementation. Merely adding a polarity description would change model metadata, not physically implement negative weights.

DC3 U1 has output SCORE_0 on pin 1, INV_L2_0 on inverting pin 2, and GND on noninverting pin 3. Passive ACT_0 through R2_0_0 into INV_L2_0 is an inverting transfer, but emulator propagate_pins computes v_score = v_act @ w2 + b2 without the negative multiplier. The score polarity is not faithful to the physical summer.

The emulator clock_spi_interface maps continuous arbitrary input pixel voltages onto shift-register output names. It does not simulate shift-register thresholds, bits, timing, PWM filtering, driver output resistance or resistor-crossbar input loading. Inference is therefore an idealized numerical transfer model, not a full pin-level circuit simulation. DC2 gain resistors are parsed but their ratio is not used in the ReLU propagation. np.clip supply saturation assumes fixed +/-10.5V headroom without a loaded-device model. None of these limitations is repaired by a successful routing conversion.
