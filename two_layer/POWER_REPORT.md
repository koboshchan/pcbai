The onboard supply now derives nominal +5V from +15V using ST L7805CV in the standard KiCad TO-220-3_Vertical footprint. Pin 1 is +15V input, pin 2 and the metal tab are GND, and pin 3 is +5V output. This is LCSC C111887, not a small 78L05. Live catalog stock was shown, but JLC assembly availability and quotation have not been confirmed. This TH regulator requires manual assembly when ordering SMD-only assembly.

The original root boards remain untouched. Only the repaired motherboard candidate has been modified. +5V powers all eight Nexperia 74AHCT595D,112 devices, selected LCSC C554704. R_REF_LINK is a mandatory 0805 zero-ohm link from +5V to the preserved +5V_REF backplane bus. The bias reference and PWM high level therefore co-vary with the regulator output. ST specifies 4.75 to 5.25V over its stated 5mA to 1A load and 7 to 18V input conditions. The model must cover that interval, with driver load droop modeled separately. The -5V_REF bus is preserved but remains unsourced and must be unused by every trained bias.

External J_REF_IN was removed. The six-pin MCU SPI header is preserved physically, with pin 1 on a unique isolated NC net. Pins 2 through 6 retain GND, MOSI, SCK, LATCH and OE. Never attach a USB or MCU 5V supply. The MCU must still supply +3V3_ADC through J_ADC_REF. All ADC clamp, divider, capacitor polarity, slot and 64 grayscale filter pad nets were checked unchanged through save and reload. All eight shift-register footprints retain their original output mapping and supply pad nets; only their part values were corrected from stale HCT to AHCT.

Regulator bypass is 0.33uF input and 0.1uF output, with 50V X7R specified. The input part must have at least 0.33uF effective capacitance at its operating voltage. A 10uF output reservoir rated at least 16V has positive pad 1 on +5V and negative pad 2 on GND. Its precise purchasing part remains to be selected. An 820ohm 5% minimum-load resistor guarantees more than 5mA at 4.75V even at +5% resistance. The existing two 100uF 50V bulk capacitors and rail bypass capacitors are preserved. The existing per-AHCT 0.1uF bypass capacitors remain necessary. Startup, supply dip and burst reservoir effectiveness require bench measurement.

Python computes 6-bit BCM clock duty as 6 planes times 64 bits divided by 20MHz, times 4800 frames/second, giving 0.09216. The audit uses AHCT CPD 190pF from the selected Nexperia datasheet, not the obsolete HCT 115pF assumption. CPD is only typical, not a guaranteed maximum. The budget conservatively includes 25 MCU-level TTL inputs at 1.5mA extra current each, 8 times 80uA static ICC, all 64 outputs sourcing up to 5.25V/9500ohm, eight serial outputs with assumed 50pF loads switching at 10MHz during shifts, the minimum-load resistor and a conservative current bound for every actual +5V_REF bias resistor on the repaired daughtercards. The filter load bound assumes filtered nodes do not fall below GND; startup/nonlinear coupled-node overshoot is not certified by this resistor-only bound. Actual serial load capacitance also requires verification.

The normal BCM estimate is 97.98mA and 1.224W regulator dissipation. Continuous 20MHz clock stress estimates 261.94mA and 3.028W. Increasing typical CPD by 50% as an engineering stress margin gives 341.74mA and 3.906W. Dissipation deliberately combines independent current and voltage corners, using +15.75V input, 4.75V output and 9.3mA regulator ground-current allowance. The allowance comprises ST's 8mA current plus published 0.5mA and 0.8mA current-change figures; its full-temperature corner is not independently guaranteed by that calculation. The +15V charger therefore needs to cover this regulator branch input current in addition to all op-amp loads.

At 50C local ambient, bare TO-220 datasheet thetaJA 50C/W predicts 201.4C junction under continuous stress and bare D2PAK thetaJA 62.5C/W predicts 239.2C. Neither is authorized without heat removal. The D2PAK estimate is already 126.5C in normal BCM under these conservative assumptions, so it was not selected.

A mandatory external heatsink with effective thetaSA no greater than 10C/W and interface resistance no greater than 2C/W, together with ST TO-220 thetaJC 5C/W, predicts 101.5C continuous junction or 116.4C with the 50% CPD margin. That is an engineering calculation, not a verified assembled thermal result. Heatsink selection, mounting envelope, daughtercard clearance, orientation and actual-enclosure thermal testing remain unresolved fabrication-release gates. The regulator is at x61mm,y96mm, with its body toward the board interior. Its footprint fits, but the larger heatsink envelope has not been established. Do not substitute an arbitrary miniature heatsink. A possible catalog family with nominal 10C/W options is https://www.mouser.com/c/thermal-management/heat-sinks/?thermal%20resistance=10%20C%2FW . No heatsink SKU has yet been selected or added to a purchase BOM. The tab is GND and conductive mounting must avoid other supply rails. The chosen C-version regulator has a 0 to125C junction operating range.
